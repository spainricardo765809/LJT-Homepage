I am a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by **Professor Junxian He**. I previously worked with Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree, where I was a recipient of the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**.
